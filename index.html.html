THE WORKFLOW

From cheque to screening report

A proposed four-stage workflow for the Cheque Guard AI system.

STEP 01 / INPUT

Capture the cheque

Obtain a cheque image through a scanner or camera and extract relevant document details.

STEP 02 / ANALYZE

Inspect key details

Use proposed OCR and computer vision components to examine cheque fields and signatures.

STEP 03 / ASSESS

Identify warning signs

Evaluate mismatches and risk indicators against authorized records and defined rules.

STEP 04 / REVIEW

Generate a report

Present findings for human review before any banking or payment decision is made.

FUTURE FEATURE

Verification reference

Generate a unique record ID or barcode linked to a verified record in a secure backend.

SECURITY FIRST

Protect sensitive data
